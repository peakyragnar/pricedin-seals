06 · What changed

Thesis update

The print was a clean beat versus both bars.

Revenue and adjusted EPS cleared the sealed medians and stayed inside the 80% ranges. They also cleared Cisco’s own May guide of $16.7–$16.9B and $1.16–$1.18.

The next-quarter guide is the larger surprise.

Q1 FY2027 revenue of $18.0–$18.2B and adjusted EPS of $1.32–$1.34 sit entirely above the sealed $16.3–$16.6B / $1.10–$1.13 expectation. That is a bigger miss for the slate than the quarter just printed.

Cash conversion ran the other way from the recent pattern.

Free cash flow of $4,996M printed $970M above the median and above the $4,750 top of the band. Five of six models were low. GPT‑5.6 Sol’s $5,000M was the only near print.

This vintage does not re-underwrite the out-years.

FY2027 guided $72.2–$73.4B and $5.05–$5.11. A post-release re-slate would be a separate timestamped vintage. This page grades the already-sealed v5 pre-print only.
